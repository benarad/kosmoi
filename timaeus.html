How large the seven circles are

Where each number comes from

    Apart from the proportions of the seven circles to one another, sizes and distances are not to scale. Timaeus gives no figure for the size of the Earth, the stars or the heaven.

    The fixed stars are the stars of the Yale Bright Star Catalogue down to magnitude 4.6, about a thousand in all, placed where they were in 350 BC. Athens is taken to be at 38° north, and the Different to be slanted at 24° to the Same.
